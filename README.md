# ALI PARTNER

> Global business platform connecting customers with trusted products, services and partners.

**Project status:** 🟡 Foundation / planning  
**Project name:** Temporary — subject to change.

## Vision

ALI PARTNER is planned as a global business discovery, marketplace and partner platform.

The platform may connect customers with products, services and business partners while supporting affiliate, referral, reseller and marketplace relationships.

This project is inspired by the general business-platform model of products such as systeme.io, but it is **not a clone**. The product structure, UX, partner model and future capabilities will be developed independently.

## Core Sections

### 01 — Home
Main landing page, positioning, featured categories, featured products and primary calls to action.

### 02 — Business Marketplace
Discover products and services from participating companies and partners.

### 03 — Categories
Organized business categories such as AI, marketing, websites, e-commerce, CRM, email, automation, design, education and productivity.

### 04 — Product Pages
Detailed product/service pages with benefits, features, pricing references, partner information and conversion paths.

### 05 — Company Pages
Profiles for participating companies, their products/services and partner information.

### 06 — Search
Fast discovery across products, services, companies and categories.

### 07 — Compare
Side-by-side comparison of relevant products and services.

### 08 — ALI Deals
Special offers, partner promotions, discounts and selected commercial opportunities.

### 09 — ALI Verified
Trust and verification layer for selected companies, products and partner information.

### 10 — Learn / Academy
Educational content, guides, tutorials and business learning resources.

### 11 — Free Business Tools
Useful lightweight calculators, generators, checklists and other client-side tools.

### 12 — Partner With ALI
Entry point for companies that want to list products/services or work with ALI.

### 13 — ALI Partner Network
Partner, affiliate and referral ecosystem connecting ALI with participating businesses.

### 14 — User Dashboard
Future authenticated area for users, saved items, activity and account features.

### 15 — Company Dashboard
Future partner/company workspace for listings, profile management and performance information.

### 16 — Commission / Earnings
Future partner reporting area for tracked commissions, referrals and earnings.

### 17 — Wallet / Payouts
Future financial layer. This will only be designed after the commercial model and real revenue are validated.

### 18 — API / Reseller / White-label
Future integration layer for APIs, reseller relationships and white-label capabilities.

## Delivery Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation & Validation 🟡
Sections **01–13**.

Goal: establish the public-facing platform structure, validate the business model and test real partner/customer demand before building a large backend.

### Phase 2 — Accounts & Operations 🔴
Sections **14–16**.

Goal: add authentication, user/company dashboards and partner performance data only when justified by real usage.

### Phase 3 — Financial & Integration Layer 🔴
Sections **17–18**.

Goal: add wallet/payout infrastructure, APIs, reseller and white-label capabilities after the platform has proven commercial demand.

## Operating Rules

1. Inspect the current state before changing anything.
2. Build in small, testable stages.
3. Do not build a large backend before business validation.
4. Do not assume partner acceptance; verify each partner program individually.
5. No fake customers, fake commissions or simulated revenue presented as real.
6. Keep this repository independent from XKiss and Alex-Bitcoin.
7. Every completed stage should be tested and reviewed before being marked **GREEN**.
8. Do not reopen a GREEN/LOCKED stage unless a real defect or regression is found.
9. The final brand name can be changed later without changing the underlying project plan.

## Status Legend

- 🟢 **GREEN** — completed and verified
- 🟡 **ACTIVE** — currently being worked on
- 🔴 **NOT STARTED** — planned for a later stage
- 🔒 **LOCKED** — reviewed and frozen unless a real issue requires reopening
